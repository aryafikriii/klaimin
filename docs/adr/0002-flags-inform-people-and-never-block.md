# Flags inform people and never block

A receipt over its category's cap, or one that looks like an earlier receipt, can still be submitted. It carries a flag that the approver sees, and a policy flag requires a justification from the claimant.

Blocking was the alternative. We rejected it because real spending has legitimate exceptions, and because duplicate detection by total and date will sometimes match two honest receipts. A person with the flag and the justification in front of them makes a better call than a hard stop.

## Consequences

Approvers carry the responsibility for flagged claims, so every decision is kept with its comment and time.
